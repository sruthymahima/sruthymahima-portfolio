The Artist

A classical dancer trained across three of India's great traditions — Bharatanatyam, Mohiniyattam and Kuchipudi — and the founder of the Omkaram School of Dance.

Sruthy Mahima is an accomplished Indian classical dancer with over twenty years of experience across Bharatanatyam, Mohiniyattam and Kuchipudi. Her practice unites the discipline of temple tradition with a deep expressive sensibility honed over two decades on stage.

Alongside performing, she has spent more than ten years teaching, nurturing a new generation of dancers as the founder of the Omkaram School of Dance. Her mission is to carry these living classical traditions to international audiences and to establish herself as an artist of exceptional promise on the world stage.
